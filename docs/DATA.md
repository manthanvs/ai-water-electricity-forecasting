## UCI Individual Household Electric Power Consumption (dataset id 235)

- Origin: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/235
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 (stated on the UCI dataset page)
- Citation: G. Hebrail and A. Berard, Individual Household Electric Power Consumption [Dataset], UCI Machine Learning Repository, 2006 (last updated 2024). DOI 10.24432/C58K54.
- Period: 2006-12-16 17:24:00 to 2010-11-26 21:02:00
- Resolution: 1 minute (2,075,259 rows)
- Units: Global_active_power in kW (minute average); Voltage in V; Global_intensity in A; Sub_metering_1..3 in Wh of active energy per minute
- Columns: Global_active_power, Global_reactive_power, Voltage, Global_intensity, Sub_metering_1, Sub_metering_2, Sub_metering_3
- Missing values: 1.25% of minutes; 71 gaps; longest 7226 minutes (5.0 days)
- Files used: `uci_electricity/household_power_consumption.zip` sha256 9f84b46ade8a...

### Known caveats

- One household in Sceaux (7 km from Paris), so the series is noisy and appliance-driven, not a feeder or city load.
- Missing values ('?') on about 1.25 percent of minutes, including multi-day outages.
- The clock has exactly 1440 minutes on every day, including daylight-saving change days, so it does not follow DST. It is treated as a fixed UTC+1 (CET) clock.
- The time zone of the clock is not documented by the source; the UTC+1 assumption matters only for aligning weather, by at most one hour.

### Use in this project

Electricity target: Global_active_power aggregated to hourly mean kW (equivalently kWh per hour) for D1, and daily totals (kWh) for W1. Other columns are not used as features.

## Battle of the Water Demand Forecasting (BWDF), WDSA-CCWI 2024

- Origin: https://github.com/WaterFutures/wf4bwdf (data bundled in the wf4bwdf package, installed through water-benchmark-hub[bwdf]); paper DOI 10.1061/JWRMD5.WRENG-6887
- Licence: Data: CC BY 4.0 (stated in the wf4bwdf README); package code: MIT
- Citation: S. Alvisi, M. Franchini, V. Marsili, F. Mazzoni, E. Salomons, et al., "Battle of Water Demand Forecasting," Journal of Water Resources Planning and Management, vol. 151, no. 10, 2025. DOI 10.1061/JWRMD5.WRENG-6887.
- Period: 2021-01-01 00:00:00+01:00 to 2023-03-31 23:00:00+02:00
- Resolution: 1 hour (19,679 rows)
- Units: DMA inflows in L/s (hourly); rainfall depth mm; air temperature deg C; humidity %; wind speed km/h
- Columns: DMA 1, DMA 2, DMA 3, DMA 4, DMA 5, DMA 6, DMA 7, DMA 8, DMA 9, DMA 10, rain_mm, temperature_c, humidity_pct, windspeed_kmh
- Missing values: inflows - DMA 1: 3.95% missing, 89 gaps, longest 74 h; DMA 2: 3.09% missing, 72 gaps, longest 71 h; DMA 3: 0.53% missing, 47 gaps, longest 31 h; DMA 4: 4.88% missing, 201 gaps, longest 75 h; DMA 5: 3.85% missing, 77 gaps, longest 74 h; DMA 6: 9.68% missing, 101 gaps, longest 1076 h; DMA 7: 7.70% missing, 160 gaps, longest 626 h; DMA 8: 5.66% missing, 45 gaps, longest 273 h; DMA 9: 7.68% missing, 41 gaps, longest 995 h; DMA 10: 4.66% missing, 110 gaps, longest 143 h. Weather missing: rain_mm 0.00%, temperature_c 0.00%, humidity_pct 4.08%, windspeed_kmh 0.14%.
- Primary series: DMA 3 (fewest missing values)
- Files used: `bwdf/InflowData.xlsx` sha256 c239bdb5c98e...; `bwdf/WeatherData.xlsx` sha256 70729f184c0b...

### Known caveats

- Ten district metered areas (DMAs) of a city in north-east Italy; the city is not named.
- Timestamps are local CET/CEST wall time: the spring hour is absent and the autumn hour appears twice (resolved in order).
- This is the complete dataset released after the competition, so it includes the four competition evaluation weeks.
- Gaps in every DMA; see Missing values above.

### Use in this project

Water target: hourly inflow of the primary DMA (fewest missing values, chosen below) for D1 and daily volume (m3) for W1. The other DMAs are kept for a robustness check. The bundled weather is used as the weather features for water.

## BattLeDIM 2020: Battle of the Leakage Detection and Isolation Methods, L-Town

- Origin: https://zenodo.org/record/4017659 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4017659)
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 (Zenodo record)
- Citation: S. G. Vrachimis, D. G. Eliades, R. Taormina, A. Ostfeld, Z. Kapelan, S. Liu, M. S. Kyriakou, P. Pavlou, M. Qiu, and M. Polycarpou, Dataset of BattLeDIM: Battle of the Leakage Detection and Isolation Methods, Zenodo, 2020. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4017659.

- Resolution: 5 minutes (105,120 rows)
- Units: Flows m3/h, tank level m, leak flows m3/h; five-minute resolution
- Columns: p227, p235, PUMP_1
- Missing values: 0.00% (max over flow columns)
- Leaks 2018: 14 leaks starting in 2018: p257 incipient 2018-01-08 13:30 to 2019-12-31 23:55 (d=11.8 mm); p461 incipient 2018-01-23 04:25 to 2018-04-02 11:40 (d=21.3 mm); p232 incipient 2018-01-31 02:35 to 2018-02-10 09:20 (d=20.1 mm); p427 incipient 2018-02-13 08:25 to 2019-12-31 23:55 (d=9.1 mm); p673 abrupt 2018-03-05 15:45 to 2018-03-23 10:25 (d=22.9 mm); p810 incipient 2018-07-28 03:05 to 2019-12-31 23:55 (d=10.0 mm); p628 incipient 2018-05-02 14:55 to 2018-05-29 21:20 (d=22.3 mm); p538 abrupt 2018-05-18 08:35 to 2018-06-02 06:05 (d=21.7 mm); p866 abrupt 2018-06-01 09:05 to 2018-06-12 03:00 (d=18.1 mm); p31 incipient 2018-06-28 10:35 to 2018-08-12 17:30 (d=16.4 mm); p654 incipient 2018-07-05 03:40 to 2019-12-31 23:55 (d=8.8 mm); p183 abrupt 2018-08-07 02:35 to 2018-09-01 17:10 (d=15.9 mm); p158 abrupt 2018-10-06 02:35 to 2018-10-23 13:35 (d=19.4 mm); p369 abrupt 2018-10-26 02:05 to 2018-11-08 20:25 (d=19.4 mm)
- Files used: `battledim/README.txt` sha256 e6a3e83bdf7f...; `battledim/dataset_configuration.yaml` sha256 23471d8989d8...; `battledim/2018_Fixed_Leakages_Report.txt` sha256 5dba254c623b...; `battledim/2018_Leakages.csv` sha256 e1abd9d549a1...; `battledim/2018_SCADA_Flows.csv` sha256 7f7e8625c9d9...; `battledim/2018_SCADA_Levels.csv` sha256 a1fc5f022944...

### Known caveats

- L-Town is a realistic benchmark network with simulated hydraulics (EPANET), not a live utility.
- Zenodo record files: README.txt, dataset_configuration.yaml, L-TOWN.inp, L-TOWN_Real.inp, 2018/2019 SCADA Flows, Levels, Pressures and Demands (CSV), 2018/2019 SCADA.xlsx, 2018/2019 Leakages.csv, 2018_Fixed_Leakages_Report.txt. Only the files listed under 'files used' were downloaded.
- Leak start, end, diameter, type (abrupt or incipient) and peak time come from dataset_configuration.yaml; several 2018 leaks run to the end of 2019 (never repaired).
- Incipient leaks grow slowly and small leaks are expected to be missed by flow-based detection.

### Use in this project

Anomaly-detection labels for water: network inflow (p227 + p235, the two reservoir inlets) aggregated to hourly, compared with the 2018 leak list.

## Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (archive)

- Origin: https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive (docs: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api)
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 (open-meteo.com/en/licence); free API for non-commercial use
- Citation: P. Zippenfenig, Open-Meteo.com Weather API, Zenodo, 2023. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7970649; underlying reanalysis: ERA5 (Hersbach et al., 2020; DOI 10.24381/cds.adbb2d47).
- Period: 2006-12-01 00:00:00+00:00 to 2010-11-30 23:00:00+00:00
- Resolution: 1 hour (35,064 rows)
- Units: temperature_2m deg C; relative_humidity_2m %; precipitation mm (hourly sums)
- Columns: temperature_c, humidity_pct, rain_mm
- Missing values: 0.00% (max over columns)
- Files used: `open_meteo/sceaux_hourly_utc_2006-12-01_2010-11-30.json` sha256 9c8f001b5813...

### Known caveats

- Reanalysis grid cell (about 48.75 N, 2.28 E, 95 m), not a station at the house.
- Requested in UTC (timezone=GMT) so that daylight saving is handled in cleaning, not by the API.
- Observed weather at the target hour stands in for a weather forecast ('perfect weather forecast' assumption; a no-weather ablation is reported).

### Use in this project

Weather features for electricity: temperature, humidity, precipitation, heating and cooling degree hours.
